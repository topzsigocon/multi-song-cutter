3. Review and edit cuts

Start and end are seconds. Change the values to fine-tune each clip. Stereo 320 kbps uses more storage and may take longer to encode on a phone.

Privacy & limitations

Audio is processed locally in your browser; this page does not upload your music. Large files may exceed your phone's memory. MP3 export uses the LAME encoder library (lame.min.js) stored with the app, so it works offline. Use only audio you have permission to edit.
